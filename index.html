Who We Are

A Grassroots Movement for a Better India

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh — operating across 28 states with a passionate network of volunteers and professionals.

We believe in the power of collective action. Every contribution — whether your time, skills, or resources — is channelled into essential causes: food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability, and social inclusion across India.

Our transparent operations — backed by 80G & 12A certifications and CSR-1 registration — enable corporate partnerships and tax-exempt donations, while our NITI Aayog registration ensures alignment with national development goals.

Our Initiatives

6 Projects Transforming Communities

Each project targets a critical social need — grounded in compassion, driven by data, and sustained by community participation.

📚
Project Bachpanshala

Nurturing Young Minds, Building Bright Futures

Bridging educational gaps by training underprivileged children in basic digital literacy, life skills, and school education support — ensuring every child gets a real chance to learn and grow.

📖 #Education · #ChildDevelopment · #DigitalLiteracy
🕊
Project Udaan

Soaring Towards a Brighter Future

Empowering women by collaborating with self-help groups in rural areas — promoting financial independence, skill development, and menstrual hygiene awareness across communities.

💪 #WomenEmpowerment · #FinancialFreedom · #RuralIndia
🐾
Project Jeev

Empowering Lives, Spreading Compassion

Dedicated to animal welfare — volunteers feed 50+ stray animals daily, conduct rescue operations, and advocate for the protection and dignity of all living beings across India.

🐕 #AnimalWelfare · #CompassionForAll · #Rescue
🤲
Project Sewa

Serving Humanity with Compassion

Over 50,000+ meals and items of clothing distributed to the underprivileged — because no one should go hungry or cold. Food and dignity delivered, one day at a time.

🍽 #FoodDistribution · #HumanitarianAid · #Sewa
🌱
Project Prakriti

Plant for a Better Tomorrow

Advocating for sustainability through planting 20,000+ saplings and supporting eco-friendly agricultural practices — protecting the environment for generations to come.

🌍 #Environment · #GreenIndia · #Sustainability
💼
Project Vikas

Skills for a Self-Reliant India

Facilitating employment through internships in data operations, finance, research, content writing, digital marketing & social work. Over 30,000+ interns trained in 4 years.

🚀 #SkillDevelopment · #Employability · #Vikas
